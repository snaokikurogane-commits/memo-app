Match a cohesive collection of softly painted soccer field, open books, mountain path, golf, travel, coffee and garden illustrations. Very gentle warm lighting, quiet intimate mood. No people or faces (animals are allowed when the subject requests them), no words, letters, logos, watermark, photorealism, 3D or generic clipart.
